Introduction

EHub66 is a Southeast Asia–focused technology group building AIoT (Artificial Intelligence of Things) platforms for mobility, transportation, and emerging smart industries.

We integrate connected devices, cloud-native SaaS platforms, and advanced AI analytics to transform fragmented operational data into real-time intelligence and predictive insights. Our solutions enable enterprises and governments to move from reactive workflows to data-driven, preventive operations at scale.

By combining hardware, connectivity, and intelligent software into a unified ecosystem, EHub66 enables continuous data collection, real-time monitoring, and automated decision-making. This empowers organizations to improve safety, increase operational efficiency, reduce costs, and unlock new digital capabilities across their operations.

Our platform-first approach allows rapid deployment across different industries while maintaining a consistent, scalable, and secure technology foundation.

Ecosystem

Provides comprehensive vehicle tracking, fleet management, and compliance solutions. Its platform enables real-time visibility into fleet operations, AI-powered driver behavior analytics, route optimization, and regulatory compliance. GPSina’s strong dealer network and nationwide presence ensure scalable distribution and customer adoption.

EHub Telematics

Acts as the core technology platform, delivering a generic, open IoT infrastructure that supports device connectivity, data ingestion, analytics pipelines, and API integrations.
