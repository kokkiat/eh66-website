EHub Group prioritizes information security to protect our customers, partners, and internal systems.

1. Security Principles

  • Access control and least-privilege policies
  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
  • Network monitoring and intrusion detection
  • Secure cloud and infrastructure management
  • Regular software updates and vulnerability management

2. Incident Management

Security incidents are monitored, investigated, and mitigated promptly.

3. Responsible Disclosure

Security researchers can report vulnerabilities to: security@ehub66.com

Researchers are requested to:

  • Limit testing to necessary scope
  • Provide detailed reports
  • Allow reasonable remediation time before public disclosure
