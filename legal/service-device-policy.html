Service & Device Policy

Last Updated: 2026-04-30

This policy applies to refunds, SIM renewal, subscriptions, delivery, installation, and device-related services for customers of EHub Group companies.

Outright Purchase

  • No cash refunds
  • Defective devices replaced per warranty

Rental Plans

  • No refunds once service starts
  • Defective devices replaced at no extra cost
  • Early termination may incur penalties

SIM Renewal & Subscription

  • Outright Purchase: yearly renewal via dealers or Customer Care
  • Rental: included in plan

Delivery & Installation

  • Dealer installation or doorstep by appointment
  • Rental: installation included per plan
  • Relocation/replacement may incur charges
